Featured games in 2026

Computer Games

League of Legends

League of Legends is a free-to-play multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game developed by Riot Games. Two teams of five players compete to destroy the opposing team's Nexus by controlling unique champions, each with their own abilities and playstyles. The game combines strategy, teamwork, and fast-paced action, making it one of the most popular esports titles in the world.

Dofus 3.0

Dofus is a tactical turn-based massively multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) featuring a colorful 2D art style and a quest to find dragon eggs. It was developed by Ankama Games.

Fornite

Fortnite is a 2017 online video game and game platform developed and released by Epic Games. It is available in seven distinct game mode versions that otherwise share the same general gameplay and game engine: Fortnite Battle Royale, a battle royale game in which up to 100 players fight to be the last person standing.


Mobile Games

Digimon Up

Digimon UP is a free-to-play idle monster-raising RPG for mobile devices released by Bandai Namco Entertainment on July 15, 2026. It features a nostalgic pixel-art style inspired by virtual pet toys and classic card games.

Flappy Bird

Flappy Bird is a viral mobile arcade game where players tap the screen to guide a bird through gaps in green pipes without crashing. Created by Dong Nguyen, it became a massive global hit in 2014 before being pulled from app stores by its creator.

Wild Rift

Wild Rift is a free-to-play 5v5 multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game. It features fast-paced matches, dual-stick mobile controls, and iconic champions adapted from the original PC game.
